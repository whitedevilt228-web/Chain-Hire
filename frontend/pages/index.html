How ChainHire Works

A transparent 3-step process connecting job seekers and verified recruiters.

1

Add Experience & Request Proof

Candidates add their previous work tenure and send a direct cryptographic verification request to their past employer.

2

Recruiter Signs On-Chain

The employer reviews the request in their inbox and verifies it with a single transaction via MetaMask using our verified smart contract.

3

Immutable Career Passport

The credential is minted permanently on-chain. Candidates apply to top Web3 jobs with 100% cryptographic proof of experience.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is on-chain employment verification? ▼

On-chain employment verification records a cryptographic attestation of a candidate's job title, company, and dates directly onto the Ethereum smart contract registry. It cannot be edited, falsified, or deleted.

Do candidates have to pay gas fees? ▼

No. Job seekers submit verification requests for free. The employer or recruiter approves the credential on-chain using their MetaMask wallet.

How does the hiring workflow manage active jobs? ▼

When an employer marks a candidate as "Hired", the system automatically marks the job position as filled and removes it from active public listings.
